# Recovery Rate Policy

## Regulatory Limits

All settlement recovery rates (RR) must fall within the allowed regulatory range:

- **Minimum RR:** 20%
- **Maximum RR:** 80%

Offers outside this range must not be recommended by the Decision Intelligence Agent.

## Approval Requirements

Recovery rates above 60% require secondary approval from a Portfolio Manager.

## Documentation

Every recommendation must record the model version, predicted probabilities, and MIP gap for audit reconstruction.
